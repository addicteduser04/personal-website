01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

Why this work exists

A Moroccan dental-supplies business needed a structured catalogue and consistent shopping flow without abandoning WhatsApp, the channel customers already used to confirm orders.

Data and source material

  • Product, category, variation, offer, campaign, university, and student-pack records in PostgreSQL
  • Original and generated card/detail images in Supabase Storage
  • Owner and administrator records protected with Supabase Auth and Row Level Security

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

What I implemented

I built a dedicated storefront and operations layer. Customers search products, browse categories and student packs, save favorites and fill a cart; the final cart becomes a structured WhatsApp order message. Administrators manage the catalogue, promotions, campaigns and manual sales.

Implemented capabilities

  • Searchable catalogue, categories and variation-aware product pages
  • Student packs grouped by university and academic year
  • Persistent cart, favorites and cart-to-WhatsApp confirmation
  • Customer profile, delivery address, notifications and order history
  • Admin workflows for products, offers, campaigns, manual sales and analytics
  • WebP image-variant pipeline with dry-run-first maintenance scripts

03 / TECHNICAL APPROACH

From input to usable output

  1. 01

    Catalogue and pack imports validate source files in dry-run mode

  2. 02

    Storefront search, categories, variations, favorites, and cart support product discovery

  3. 03

    Cart contents become a structured WhatsApp message

  4. 04

    Administrators manage products, campaigns, student packs, manual sales, and analytics

04 / METHODOLOGY & IMPLEMENTATION

Decisions that shape the system

  • Next.js 16 App Router separates storefront, account, and administrator workflows.
  • Zustand keeps the browser cart responsive while PostgreSQL remains authoritative for durable records.
  • Maintenance scripts default to dry-run and require explicit production confirmation before writes.
  • An image pipeline creates WebP card and detail variants to control storage egress.

05 / RESULTS & EVIDENCE

What the source supports

  • The repository documents a production catalogue used by a real business.
  • The implemented flow preserves WhatsApp confirmation while adding searchable product discovery and structured administration.
  • No revenue, order-volume, or conversion claim is publicly documented.

Results preserve the repository’s units, evaluation framing, and stated limitations. Missing evidence is not converted into a claim.

06 / CHALLENGES & LIMITATIONS

Where the evidence stops

  • Checkout intentionally ends in WhatsApp; it is not an online-payment system.
  • Operational results depend on the business maintaining catalogue data and media.
  • The public case study omits customer records, credentials, and private business data.
MISSING EVIDENCE

No public conversion, revenue, or order-volume evidence. Customer and operational datasets remain confidential.

07 / WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Engineering signal

Translating an existing sales process into a production product without forcing the business to abandon the channel its customers already understand.
