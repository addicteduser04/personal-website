01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

Why this work exists

Moroccan investors need portfolio tracking and research tooling that respects Casablanca-market data constraints instead of silently fabricating unavailable prices, fundamentals, or benchmark history.

Data and source material

  • Immutable cash, trade, dividend, fee, and tax transactions
  • Published or provisional OHLCV records and Moroccan security metadata
  • Optional MASI-family indices, company documents, annual reports, and fundamentals when rights and data are available

02 / SOLUTION OVERVIEW

From input to usable output

The platform separates an immutable transaction ledger from derived analytics, supports real-tracking and simulation portfolios, and makes missing or stale data explicit.

  1. 01

    Record real-tracking or simulation transactions

  2. 02

    Replay the ledger with exact-decimal weighted-average-cost accounting

  3. 03

    Build derived snapshots through locked, retryable worker jobs

  4. 04

    Value positions and calculate performance only when required market data exists

  5. 05

    Stage and publish prices through a distinct second-admin review

04 / METHODOLOGY & IMPLEMENTATION

Decisions that shape the system

  • The transaction ledger is authoritative; corrections are immutable counter-entries visible at correction time.
  • Derived snapshots are disposable caches and cannot replace a newer valid generation.
  • TWR and XIRR cover multiple periods; risk and concentration views appear only with sufficient data.
  • Owner isolation uses PostgreSQL Row Level Security, while public-safe views separate published data from private ingestion tables.

Implemented capabilities

  • Real-tracking and simulation portfolios
  • Weighted-average-cost accounting and deterministic replay
  • TWR/XIRR, portfolio risk and concentration analytics
  • Moroccan security directory with OHLCV charting
  • Two-admin price staging/publication
  • Optional AI explanations with deterministic fallback

05 / RESULTS & EVIDENCE

What the source supports

  • The repository documents a final MVP spanning authentication, ledger accounting, CSV import, valuation, performance, market research, and administration.
  • English, French, and Arabic—including right-to-left presentation—are in scope.
  • Market facts remain explicitly unavailable when a trusted source or redistribution right is missing.

Results preserve the repository’s units, evaluation framing, and stated limitations. Missing evidence is not converted into a claim.

06 / CHALLENGES & LIMITATIONS

Where the evidence stops

  • SaifInvest is not a brokerage or custodian and never represents recorded transactions as market orders.
  • Production market-data display depends on appropriate exchange and redistribution rights.
  • Optional AI explanation has a deterministic fallback and is not the source of financial calculations.
MISSING EVIDENCE

No public production-user metrics; exchange-data rights and some external services are environment-dependent.

07 / WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Engineering signal

Financial domain modelling, exact accounting, data governance, multilingual product engineering, and explicit handling of incomplete market data.
